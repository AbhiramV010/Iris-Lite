Mirrored values

FRAME_BUFFER_SIZE and SLOT_SIZE exist independently in Python (main.py) and C++ (SharedFrameBuffer.hpp, as BUFFER_SIZE and SLOT_SIZE). Nothing checks that they agree. Change one without the other and the compressor reads at the wrong stride, producing decode failures rather than an error.

Event schema

The record every detector sends over the event bus. Defined in Decision/captureinfo.py and pickled by multiprocessing.connection.

FieldTypeContents
startTimestrHH:MM:SS, already padded 5 s earlier than the real start.
endTimestrHH:MM:SS, already padded 5 s later.
triggerstrFree text. tiered_cap, Grass Overlap, or a label with confidence such as Gunshot (81.2%).
durationfloat?Seconds. Fixed at 10.0 for motion; measured for zone and audio.
isMotionSensorbool?PIR on BCM 27 fired within the last 10 s. Swapped with the door field in zone_monitor.py — see known issues.
isDoorSensorbool?Reed switch on BCM 17 fired within the last 10 s.

Dates are not carried. main.py combines the received time with datetime.date.today(), so an event spanning midnight resolves incorrectly.

Clip naming

iris_<startFrame>_<endFrame>_<trigger>.mp4

# e.g. iris_3204_3684_unknown.mp4

Frame numbers are ring indices, not timestamps, so they wrap every five minutes and are not globally ordered.
